# BioGas Inc.

By Mohammed Anwar. [Visit the website](https://mohammedmahram.blog/biogas-inc/).

The narrated 3D walkthrough follows livestock manure from the pen to the kitchen
and the field. Both the [English](index.html) and [Arabic](ar.html) pages include
a Yemen household feasibility summary, sourcing evidence and six livestock charts
with their values in accessible tables. The [full study](research/study-ar.html)
and [original Markdown](research/BioYemen_Feasibility_Study_AR.md) are in Arabic.

The supplied charts credit FAOSTAT 2024 for livestock counts. The regional model
estimates gas at full collection and at 30% manure collection, with 1.5 m³/day
per household cooking equivalent. The household study separately assumes 80%
use of the gas produced by one digester.

3D scene by three.js. Narration generated with VoiceStudio.
